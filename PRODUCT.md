# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS, no frameworks or external libraries. Published on GitHub Pages from the `main` branch of Juanpage/hexnex (https://juanpage.github.io/hexnex/).

## Users

Owners of small businesses in Ecuador, of any industry (hotels, restaurants, shops, clinics, academies, professional services, NGOs, tourism). They are not technical. They arrive from a WhatsApp conversation, a referral, or Meta's business profile, and want to know quickly whether Hexnex can help them get more inquiries, sales, bookings or appointments.

## Product Purpose

Hexnex is a web development and custom systems brand based in Quito. The site presents its services and moves visitors to start a conversation on WhatsApp, where the Hexnex assistant takes over. It also serves as the business website Meta requires for the WhatsApp Business account. Success: a visitor writes on WhatsApp.

## Positioning

Hexnex builds to measure: own websites with quotes, bookings or orders; WhatsApp assistants that answer 24 hours a day and register leads; custom systems; monthly maintenance. It already built WhatsApp assistants for tourism agencies (Visiting Galapagos and Travel Sant). Data is handled under Ecuador's personal data protection law (LOPDP).

## Product Principles

1. Every section moves the visitor one step closer to writing on WhatsApp.
2. Speak to non-technical owners: outcomes for their business, not technology.
3. Show only real proof; honesty over impressive claims.
4. The site itself is the portfolio piece: it must show the custom craft Hexnex sells.
